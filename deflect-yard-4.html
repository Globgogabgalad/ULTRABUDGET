Deflect Yard

An open yard to practice in. Summon enemies, then use the revolver and its coin, the pump shotgun, or the rocket launcher, and punch their shots back at whoever fired them. Play alone or with friends on your LAN.

Summon enemies

Punch just as a shot reaches you. The first instant of the swing is a perfect parry: the world freezes for a beat, and the shot comes back faster, hits harder, and homes in. Orange shots are explosive: punch them back, or shoot them in the air.
